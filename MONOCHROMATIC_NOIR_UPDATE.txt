### Brand Enforcement
✅ Strict Monochromatic Design System Enforced
- No indigo/blue/colored active states
- Pure white/greyscale/black palette only
- White shining glow effects for active states
- Consistent hover behavior across all components
- SVG topology uses #808080 (medium grey) for all connectors

### Design Rationale
1. **White Shining** = Active/Selected state indicator (high opacity, glow)
2. **Greyscale (#808080)** = Neutral topology structure
3. **Dark/Black** = Inactive/background states
4. **Noir Aesthetic** = Professional, cinematic, high-contrast
